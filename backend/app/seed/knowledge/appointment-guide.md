# How to book an appointment

## How can I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment in three ways:
- Call the front desk on +91 80 0123 4567 during opening hours (Monday to Saturday).
- Email hello@cedarline.example with your name, phone number, the doctor or service you need and two or three preferred dates. The front desk will reply to confirm a time.
- Visit the reception desk at the clinic in person.

The "How to book" page of our website (/appointments) lists these options together with each doctor's consultation days.

## Can I book online?
The website does not take bookings directly. Please call, email or visit the front desk, and the team will find a suitable time with the right doctor for you. The website assistant can help you choose the right department or doctor and find their consultation days before you call.

## What information do I need to give?
Please give your full name, a phone number we can reach you on, the doctor or service you need and your preferred dates and times. Please do not send detailed medical history by email; your doctor will discuss it with you during the consultation.

## How do I know my appointment is confirmed?
The front desk confirms your appointment date and time on the phone, or replies to your email with the confirmed time. If you have not heard back within one working day, please call the clinic.

## Can I choose my doctor?
Yes. You can ask for a specific doctor when you book. Each doctor consults on particular days, which are listed on the Our Doctors page (/doctors) and on the How to book page (/appointments).

## Rescheduling and cancelling
Please let us know at least 24 hours in advance if you need to reschedule or cancel, by phone or email. This allows us to offer the time to another patient.

## Arriving for your appointment
Please arrive about 10 minutes before your appointment time to complete registration. Bring a photo ID, any previous reports or prescriptions, and a list of medicines you currently take. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your visit.
